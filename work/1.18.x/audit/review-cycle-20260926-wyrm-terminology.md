# Review Cycle 2026-09-26: `Wyrm` terminology

## Rule

- Do not replace `Wyrm` mechanically.
- Neutral creature names use `웜(Wyrm)` for named units and `웜` or
  `뱀룡` in descriptive text.
- When a speaker uses `wyrm` to belittle another creature, inspect the
  speaker and situation. In confirmed insult contexts, use `도마뱀` or
  `도마뱀들`.

- Neutral unit names and descriptions retain `웜(Wyrm)`, `웜`, or `뱀룡`.
